# Devlog Summary

- 2026-09-06: **The repo joins the fleet and gets a design** — `cards` had been failing fleet's census gate as `NOT IN THE CENSUS`, so it would never have received the canon at all; adding it took `sync-canon` from 23 to **24 current** with `npm test` 84/84 (`fleet` `a7bdf58`, `cards` `d6422a0`). Its AGENTS.md was authored around a block rendered by fleet's own `canon.mjs`, so it classified `current` on the first check rather than after a repair. The design was then settled by five rounds of structured interview and written to `docs/design/game.md`: manual placement with automatic left-to-right resolution, random targeting with Guard as the only redirect, an equipped hero acting last, unlimited board width, units/spells/equipment, class-based characters over racial tribes, and sigils carrying both card modification and the run-wide effects that would otherwise have been a separate relic system. Two owner answers changed the shape more than their length suggests. **Random targeting** dissolved the largest risk in the first draft, where lane targeting and cascade order were two positional systems competing for one placement decision — placement now means cascade only. **Unlimited board width** was assessed as high-risk and that assessment was wrong: the owner pointed out units require cards, which require drafting, and the arithmetic agrees — energy, hand size and deck composition cap the board near six to nine units in play and about fifteen at the extreme, so the board self-limits at roughly twice Hearthstone's width and deck design is the real dial. What survived the correction is narrower and more useful: **token generation** is the only route to a genuinely unbounded line, because summoned bodies bypass all three gates, so the budget discipline belongs there and not on the board rule. Cascade traits are adjacency-only for the same reason — a counting trait's ceiling is whatever the deck economy currently allows, which is not a number anyone controls directly. Nothing is prototyped; every number in the design is a reasoned starting guess and the document's first line says so.
